📚 How to Calculate Percentage?

Percentage കണക്കാക്കാനുള്ള formula:

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example:
Obtained Marks = 450
Total Marks = 500

Percentage = (450 ÷ 500) × 100 = 90%

🎓 How to Calculate CGPA?

CGPA എന്നത് വിദ്യാർത്ഥിയുടെ subjects-ലെ grade points-ന്റെ ശരാശരിയാണ്.

CGPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Number of Subjects

Example:
Grade Points = 8, 9, 7, 8
Number of Subjects = 4

CGPA = (8 + 9 + 7 + 8) ÷ 4 = 8 CGPA

Attendance percentage കണക്കാക്കാനുള്ള formula:

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Attendance = (45 ÷ 50) × 100 = 90%
